How to Download.

Steps to Download:

▪ Step 1: First, from the specific content page on the bottmovies site, click on the download link button to click on Download File / Zip or copy the link.
▪ Step 2: If you clicked the download file/zip button, you need to copy the terabox link from your browser.
▪ Step 3: Go back to the bottmovies website, navigate to that content again, click the server-1 or server-2 button, and then click the downloader web button.
▪ Step 4: Now, paste the copied url/link and click the download button.
▪ Step 5: After clicking the download button, the link will be processed. Click on Download below to start your download.
Note, if you face any Problem please comment down.
